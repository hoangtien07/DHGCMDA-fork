This updated version further introduces an additional association type (tissue) and
adopts more refined and accurate criteria for evidence classification.
In this study, an identical standardized preprocessing pipeline is implemented for
both HMDD v2.0 and HMDD v3.2 to ensure data density and reliability. Specifically,
miRNAs and diseases with fewer than two experimentally verified associations are
excluded, and the remaining data subsets are utilized for experiments, with detailed
statistics summarized in Table 1 and Table 2. As shown in Table 2, the distribution
of association types exhibits notable imbalance in both datasets. In HMDD v2.0,
genetics accounts for the largest proportion (40.56%), whereas epigenetics represents
only 11.85%. In HMDD v3.2, the imbalance is even more pronounced, with epigenetics
constituting merely 3.43% of all associations compared to 34.02% for target type. This
substantial class imbalance motivates the adoption of class-weighted cross-entropy loss
(Eq. 34) to prevent the model from being biased toward majority types.
3.2 Evaluation settings
Two types of 5-fold cross-validation were employed to comprehensively evaluate
method performance in predicting miRNA-disease association types.
