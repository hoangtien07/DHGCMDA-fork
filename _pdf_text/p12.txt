2.4 Contrastive Learning Hypergraph Convolutional Network
Contrastive learning has emerged as a powerful paradigm for enhancing graph
embedding quality in supervised learning scenarios. By optimizing contrastive objec-
tives designed to discriminate between positive and negative sample pairs, highly
discriminative and robust graph feature representations are learned. Hence, a hyper-
graph contrastive learning method is proposed herein, which capitalizes on both
intra-modality consistency across complementary views and cross-modality alignment
between miRNAs and diseases.
