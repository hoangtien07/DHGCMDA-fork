• CVtriplet: All known miRNA-disease-type triplets were randomly partitioned into
5 equal subsets. In each iteration, one subset combined with an equal number of
randomly sampled unknown triplets served as the test set, while the remaining four
subsets along with other unknown samples constituted the training set. Method
performance was evaluated using the area under the precision-recall curve (AUPR),
the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC), and F1-score.
This evaluation setting assesses the capability of DHGCMDA in discovering novel
miRNA-disease associations from the vast space of unexplored pairs.
• CVtype: All miRNA-disease pairs with at least one known association type were
identified and randomly divided into 5 subsets. In each fold, four subsets were used
for training while the remaining subset was held out for testing. For each test pair,
DHGCMDA generated prediction scores across all four association types, with the
highest-scoring type selected as the final prediction. Top-1 precision, Top-1 recall,
and Top-1 F1-score were calculated to evaluate discriminative performance. This
evaluation setting focuses on the discriminative ability of DHGCMDA in identifying
specific biological mechanisms underlying known associations.
These two complementary evaluation settings assess performance from both asso-
ciation discovery and mechanism identification perspectives, consistent with dual
objectives emphasized in recent miRNA-disease association type prediction studies.
