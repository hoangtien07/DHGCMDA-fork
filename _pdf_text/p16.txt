2.5 Attention-Guided Adaptive View Fusion
Different similarity views exhibit heterogeneous contributions to association type pre-
diction, with their relative importance varying across distinct biological contexts. To
address this issue, an attention-guided adaptive view fusion is applied to dynamically
weight the contribution of each view.

the contrastive learning HGCN, an attention mechanism is employed to adaptively
integrate these embeddings. Specifically, for the v-th view where v ∈ {1, 2}, a global

formulated as,
