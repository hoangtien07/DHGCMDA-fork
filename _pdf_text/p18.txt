This reconstruction objective serves multiple purposes: it acts as a regularizer to
mitigate overfitting, provides a quantitative means to validate the quality of learned
representations, and critically, the reconstructed similarity matrices are leveraged to
dynamically update the heterogeneous graph structure during training, a strategy that
implements graph regularization, enabling DHGCMDA to refine its structural assump-
tions as the quality of representations improves while imposing structural consistency
constraints between the embedding space and graph space.
2.7 Heterogeneous Graph Transformer
After dual-view feature extraction and fusion, a heterogeneous graph G = ( V, E, R)
is constructed to explicitly model relationships among miRNAs, diseases, and their
associations. The node set V = M∪D consists of miRNA nodes and disease nodes, with
each node assigned the corresponding fused embeddingsZM and ZD, respectively. The
edge set E encompasses four distinct types, namely, miRNA-miRNA similarity edges
weighted by bSM, disease-disease similarity edges weighted by bSD, miRNA-disease
association edges, and disease-miRNA association edges.
To enable type-aware message passing across this heterogeneous structure, a HGT
architecture is employed. Unlike homogeneous GNNs, HGT incorporates type-specific
parameter settings to capture distinct semantic roles of different biological entities.
