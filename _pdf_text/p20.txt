With two stacked HGT layers and four attention heads per layer, through multi-
layer type-aware message passing, DHGCMDA progressively refines node embeddings
by integrating multi-hop heterogeneous neighborhood information across distinct bio-
logical entity types. The refined final embeddings of miRNA and disease nodes are
subsequently fed into a bilinear predictor, which enables simultaneous prediction of
two critical tasks: the existence of miRNA-disease associations and the corresponding
functional types underlying these associations.
2.8 Dynamic Hypergraph Update Strategy
To enable co-evolution of graph structure and learned representations, a dynamic
hypergraph update strategy is introduced. Initially, the graph is constructed using raw
similarity matrices retrieved from biological databases, which inevitably contain mea-
surement noise. As training proceeds and node embeddings are progressively refined,
the reconstructed similarity matrices bSM and bSD can better characterize the true
underlying biological relationships.
