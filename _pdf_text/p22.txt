3 Results and Discussion
3.1 Dataset
The Human MicroRNA Disease Database (HMDD) serves as a widely adopted bench-
mark resource for miRNA-disease association prediction [17]. HMDD v2.0 has been
extensively leveraged as a foundational data source in computational studies dedicated
to this task, in which curated associations are classified into four distinct types based
on evidence from genetics, epigenetics, circulation, and miRNA-target interactions.
Building on this classification framework, HMDD v3.2 delivers substantial expansions
in dataset coverage, with more miRNAs, diseases, and validated associations included.
