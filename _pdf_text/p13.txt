The contrastive learning HGCN architecture consists of two parallel HGCN

effective contrastive optimization.
2.4.1 Intra-modality Contrastive Learning
For any node vi, its embedding generated from one view, denoted as ui, is designated
as the anchor. The counterpart embedding of node vi derived from the other view is
represented as vi. Specifically, the embedding pair ui and vi of the same node across
two views constitutes a positive sample pair. In contrast, ui and vi are treated as
negative samples when paired with all other embeddingsuk and vk (k ̸= i) from either
of the two views. Notably, the embedding uk of other nodes within the same view as
the anchor ui forms intra-view negative pairs with ui, whereas the embedding vk of
other nodes from the opposite view forms inter-view negative pairs with ui.
