3.4 Comparison with other methods
To evaluate the performance of DHGCMDA, comparative experiments were conducted
against six state-of-the-art methods for miRNA-disease association type prediction,
namely TDRC [22], SPLDHyperAWNTF [24], TFLP [25], NMCMDA [28], MRFG-
MDA [33], and KBLTDARD [34]. The experiments were carried out on the HMDD
v3.2 and HMDD v2.0 datasets under the CV type and CV triplet evaluation settings.
The comparative results are presented in Table 3 and Table 4, respectively.
As shown in Table 3, DHGCMDA significantly outperforms all methods on HMDD
v3.2 and achieves competitive performance on HMDD v2.0 under the CV type. Specif-
ically, on HMDD v3.2, DHGCMDA delivers a Top-1 precision of 0.7915, recall of
0.9421 and F1-score of 0.8600, with respective gains of 25.61%, 45.36% and 35.75%
over the suboptimal values of each metric derived from different comparison methods.
On HMDD v2.0, DHGCMDA achieves the highest Top-1 recall among all methods.
While its precision lags behind that of several comparison methods, DHGCMDA still
maintains a top-tier F1-score. These remarkable gains on HMDD v3.2 stem from dual-
view hypergraph and contrastive learning modules that effectively capture high-order
biological relationships, advantages single-graph or linear methods lack when handling
complex data distributions. The moderate precision on HMDD v2.0 is a deliberate
trade-off via class weighting, which prioritizes true association recall to ensure optimal
F1-score balance for biomedical prediction tasks.
