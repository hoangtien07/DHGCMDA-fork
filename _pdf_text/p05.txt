More recently, graph neural network (GNN) methods have demonstrated superior per-
formance by explicitly modeling the topological relationships among biological entities
through message passing mechanisms, and have been widely applied to miRNA-disease
association prediction. Lu et al. [12] proposed a hypergraph learning method HGT-
MDA that integrates an improved graph convolutional network (GCN)-Transformer
encoder with a restart random walk strategy for predicting miRNA-disease associ-
ations. Liu et al. [13] developed GCNPCA, which combines GCN with attention
mechanisms for topological feature extraction and fuses node attributes derived from
principal component analysis (PCA) to support miRNA-disease association prediction.
Ma et al. [14] introduced a self-feature-based graph autoencoder SFGAE designed to
address excessive smoothing in deep graph models while enabling miRNA-disease asso-
ciation prediction. Li et al. [15] proposed an ensemble model GCSENet that integrates
GCN, convolutional neural networks, and squeeze-and-excitation networks to achieve
feature aggregation from heterogeneous networks for miRNA-disease association pre-
diction. Ning et al. [16] developed HHAWMD, which employs hierarchical hypergraph
learning in association-weighted heterogeneous networks to capture high-order depen-
dencies among biological entities for miRNA-disease association identification. These
methods, despite their demonstrated efficacy in binary association prediction, predom-
inantly frame miRNA-disease relationships as simple presence-absence links, thereby
overlooking the diverse biological mechanisms that underpin such associations. Accu-
mulating evidence indicates that miRNA dysregulation drives disease pathogenesis via
diverse underlying molecular mechanisms [17]. For instance, miR-146a exhibits dual
roles in gastric cancer, promoting cancer progression via direct targeting of the SMAD4
gene to modulate cell proliferation and apoptosis [18], while its ectopic expression
exerts tumor-suppressive effects by suppressing gastric cancer cell motility and reduc-
ing both EGFR and IRAK1 expression [19]. This mechanistic heterogeneity highlights
