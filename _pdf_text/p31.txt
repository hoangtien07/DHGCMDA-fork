3.6 Case Study
To further evaluate the capability of DHGCMDA in predicting novel miRNA-disease
association types, case studies were conducted on breast neoplasms and hepatocellular
carcinoma. DHGCMDA, trained on the HMDD v2.0 dataset, was employed to predict
miRNA-disease-type triplets. For each disease, miRNA-type pairs were ranked by their
prediction scores, with the top 15 ones selected as candidates. Supporting evidence
for these candidates was retrieved and verified via extensive literature searches in the
PubMed database, and validation results are summarized in Table 5 and Table 6,
corresponding to breast neoplasms and hepatocellular carcinoma, respectively.
For breast neoplasms, 13 out of 15 predicted miRNA-disease association types
(86.7% validation rate) were successfully confirmed by published studies. For instance,
hsa-mir-148a, whose epigenetics type association was validated by evidence that it reg-
ulates estrogen receptor-α expression through DNMT1-mediated DNA methylation in
breast cancer cells (PMID: 24257477); hsa-mir-195, identified as a reliable circulation
biomarker with significantly elevated levels in the plasma of breast cancer patients
compared with healthy controls (PMID: 25103018); hsa-mir-125b, whose genetics type
association was established via the discovery of a risk variant in the BMPR1B 3’UTR
binding site that disrupts miRNA binding and increases cancer susceptibility (PMID:
19738052); and hsa-mir-1290, confirmed as a target type miRNA that directly tar-
gets NAT1 and exhibits subtype-specific downregulation in ER-positive breast tumors
(PMID: 23183268).
