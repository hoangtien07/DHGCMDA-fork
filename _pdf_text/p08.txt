association prediction remains insufficiently explored.
To address these challenges, this study presents a dual-view heterogeneous graph
contrastive learning framework, abbreviated as DHGCMDA, for the prediction of
miRNA-disease association types. Accordingly, the main contributions of DHGCMDA
are fourfold:
• Dual-view hypergraphs are constructed based on heterogeneous similarity data, thus
avoiding excessive reliance on association-derived similarity metrics and enabling
robust prediction for novel miRNAs without prior disease annotations.
• A hypergraph convolutional network (HGCN) is employed to capture high-order
topological relationships between miRNAs and diseases, beyond traditional pairwise
connections, by leveraging hyperedges to model multi-way dependencies in biological
systems.
• Hypergraph convolution is integrated with contrastive learning to enhance repre-
sentation learning, where intra-modality contrastive learning enforces consistency
across complementary views of the same entity and cross-modality contrastive
learning aligns embedding spaces of miRNAs and diseases.
