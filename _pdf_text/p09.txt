2 Methods
2.1 Overview of DHGCMDA
The overview of DHGCMDA is illustrated in Fig. 1. First, dual-view hypergraphs
corresponding to miRNAs and diseases are constructed based on heterogeneous
similarity data. Second, a HGCN is employed to capture high-order topological rela-
tionships between miRNAs and diseases, with hypergraph convolution integrated with
contrastive learning, where intra-modality contrastive learning ensures cross-view con-
sistency and cross-modality contrastive learning aligns embedding spaces, to enhance
feature representation quality. Finally, an attention-guided adaptive view fusion strat-
egy is utilized to dynamically weight and integrate distinct view representations, while
type-aware message passing implemented via a HGT enables simultaneous prediction
of association presence and functional types.
2.2 Problem Formulation and Data Representation
The prediction of miRNA-disease associations is formulated as a link prediction prob-
lem in a heterogeneous biological network. Let M = {m1, m2, . . . , mnm} denote the
set of nm miRNAs, and D = {d1, d2, . . . , dnd} denote the set of nd diseases. Their
association relationships are encoded in a matrix A ∈ Rnm×nd, where each entry Aij
quantifies the association between miRNA mi and disease dj. For binary association
prediction, Aij takes a binary value of either 1 or 0, where 1 indicates a confirmed
association between mi and dj. It should be noted that Aij = 0 does not imply
