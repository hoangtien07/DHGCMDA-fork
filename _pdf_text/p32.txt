Notably, two breast neoplasms associated and three hepatocellular carcinoma asso-
ciated miRNAs were labeled as “Unconfirmed”. This designation does not indicate
flaws in the model predictions; instead, it reflects the absence of direct evidence sup-
porting these specific functional types in published literature. In fact, all these miRNAs
have been confirmed to be associated with their corresponding cancers, except that
their actual association types are inconsistent with the model predictions. This phe-
nomenon suggests that these unconfirmed associations may represent potential novel
miRNA-disease-type triplets, which need further targeted experimental validation.
Specifically, these predictions could constitute biologically plausible novel discoveries
that merit prioritized wet-lab validation, such as qRT-PCR verification of circulating
miRNA levels or functional assays to confirm the predicted mechanism types. Alter-
natively, a proportion of these cases may reflect model uncertainty in distinguishing
closely related association types, particularly when the biological boundaries between
mechanisms are ambiguous. Nevertheless, the small proportion of unconfirmed cases
(5 out of 30 total predictions, 16.7%) suggests that the overall prediction reliability of
DHGCMDA remains high.
