2.3 Dual-View Hypergraph Construction
Traditional pairwise graph structures exhibit inherent limitations in capturing the
complex multi-way dependencies prevalent in biological systems. By contrast, hyper-
graphs enable more expressive representations via hyperedges that connect arbitrary
node subsets, thus facilitating the modeling of higher-order relationships. For each
of these four augmented views, a hypergraph H = ( V, E, W) is constructed, where
V denotes the node set, E represents the hyperedge set, and W assigns importance
weights to individual hyperedges.
Specifically, a K-nearest neighbors (KNN) strategy is adopted to generate locality-
preserving hyperedges from augmented feature matrices. For each node vi, its K
nearest neighbors are identified based on cosine similarity in the augmented feature
space, and a hyperedge ei = {vi} ∪ KN N(vi, K) is constructed accordingly. The
hypergraph structure is formally encoded using an incidence matrix H ∈ {0, 1}n×|E|,
